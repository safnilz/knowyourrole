KNOW YOUR ROLE

FOOD IS NOT WASTE® Simulation

Find your assigned role below. During the simulation, think and make decisions as this actor.

SIMULATION ROLES
ROLE 01

Producer

“You grow, manufacture or prepare food before it moves through the system.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Demand
  • Production
  • Quality
  • Shelf life
  • Avoiding overproduction
THINK

“What should we produce, and what happens if demand changes?”

ROLE 02

Distributor

“You store and move food between organisations.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Inventory
  • Transport
  • Timing
  • Storage
  • Cold chain
  • Delivery commitments
THINK

“Where is the food now, and can it be stopped or redirected?”

ROLE 03

Retailer

“You make food available for sale to consumers.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Customer demand
  • Stock levels
  • Shelf life
  • Storage
  • Unsold products
THINK

“Can we sell or redirect this before it loses value?”

ROLE 04

Hospitality

“You prepare and serve food through hotels, restaurants, catering or events.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Guest numbers
  • Preparation
  • Food safety
  • Service
  • Cost
  • Surplus
THINK

“What can we stop, change or redirect if demand suddenly changes?”

ROLE 05

Redistribution Partner

“You help safe, edible surplus food reach people who can use it.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Food safety
  • Quantity
  • Timing
  • Collection
  • Storage
  • Recipient capacity
THINK

“Can this food still safely feed people?”

ROLE 06

Recovery Partner

“You help recover value when food or material can no longer serve its original purpose.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Material type
  • Condition
  • Contamination
  • Volume
  • Available recovery routes
THINK

“If this cannot stay as food, what is the next-best use?”
