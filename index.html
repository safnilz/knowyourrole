KNOW YOUR ROLE

FOOD IS NOT WASTE® Simulation

Select the role assigned to you. During the simulation, think and make decisions as this actor.

SIMULATION ROLES

Local Producer

“You grow or produce food locally and supply it into the food system.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Production
  • Demand
  • Quality
  • Shelf life
  • Forecasting
  • Surplus
THINK

“How much should we produce, and what happens if demand changes?”

Importer / Manufacturer

“You import food or ingredients, or manufacture food products before they move through the market.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Demand forecasting
  • Inventory
  • Production
  • Shelf life
  • Supply commitments
  • Surplus
THINK

“What should we bring into the system, and what happens if demand is lower than expected?”

Distributor / Cold Chain

“You store and move food between organisations while maintaining the conditions needed to protect its quality and safety.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Inventory
  • Transport
  • Timing
  • Storage
  • Cold chain
  • Delivery commitments
THINK

“Where is the food now, and can we stop, store or redirect it before value is lost?”

Retailer

“You make food available for sale to consumers.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Customer demand
  • Stock levels
  • Shelf life
  • Storage
  • Promotions
  • Unsold products
THINK

“Can we sell or redirect this food before it loses value?”

Hospitality / F&B

“You purchase, prepare and serve food through hotels, restaurants, catering or other food-service operations.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Guest demand
  • Purchasing
  • Preparation
  • Food safety
  • Service
  • Surplus
THINK

“What can we stop, change or redirect if demand suddenly changes?”

Corporate / Institutional Buyer

“You purchase food or catering services for an organisation, workplace, school, hospital, event or other institution.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Headcount
  • Ordering
  • Budgets
  • Service requirements
  • Demand forecasting
  • Unused food
THINK

“Are we ordering what we actually need, and what can we change if demand changes?”

Redistribution Partner

“You help safe, edible surplus food reach people who can use it instead of allowing it to become waste.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Food safety
  • Quantity
  • Timing
  • Collection
  • Storage
  • Recipient capacity
THINK

“Can this food still safely feed people, and can we move it in time?”

Circular Resource Partner

“You find productive uses for food or materials that can no longer remain in their original use.”

YOUR FOCUS
  • Material type
  • Condition
  • Contamination
  • Volume
  • Processing options
  • Recovery value
THINK

“If this cannot remain in its original use, what is the best next use for it?”
